The community pact

Anonymity grants freedom. A supportive space relies on respect.

  1. Keep it empatheticBehind every unverified name is a real person looking for perspective.
  2. Protect real privacyNever post contact details, real names or identifying information about yourself or anyone else.
  3. Zero maliceHate speech, targeted harassment and threats are not tolerated.
